What Happened?

Yami accidentally fell from the 6th floor. Despite the severity of the fall, no vital organs were damaged, which gives us real hope. However, he suffered several fractures: to his jaw, elbow, and ribs.

The veterinarian clearly explained the situation: we had two choices. Let him go... or try to save him. We couldn't bring ourselves to let him die. He's young, he wants to live, and he'll celebrate his 1st birthday on May 24th.

So we're fighting, even though it's hard. The treatment costs at least €7,688! We've already put €1,500 of our own money, doing everything we can... but we can't do it alone.

It's a matter of life or death for Yami. That's why we're launching this fundraiser. If you can make a donation, even a small one, every euro is an extra chance for him.

To all those who think €1 doesn't count, you're wrong. Every donation, no matter how small, makes a difference. If everyone gave just €1, we'd reach our goal. Never underestimate the impact of your support!
